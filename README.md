
# GridPulse

### Power Your Journey. Share the Energy.

GridPulse is an EV Charging Network and Community Charging Platform that connects electric vehicle owners with publicly available and privately shared charging stations.

The platform allows users to discover charging stations, reserve charging slots, track charging sessions, and manage charging expenses.

Charging point owners can share their home charging infrastructure with other EV users during selected available time slots.

## Features

- User authentication and profile management
- Electric vehicle registration and management
- Public and community home charging stations
- Station discovery and filtering
- Charging slot reservation
- Peak and off-peak electricity pricing
- Charging session simulation
- Automatic charging cost calculation
- Booking history and billing records
- Station host management and earnings
- Administrator dashboard and analytics
